Chapter 1 Background of the System

1.1 Type of System (Online System / Standalone System)

The 88 Speedmart System is built as an online, centralized client-server database system rather than a standalone desktop application. In a retail chain setup where multiple physical branches, regional warehouses, and online shopping channels operate simultaneously, choosing an online architecture is an operational necessity. If the company were to deploy a standalone system, every physical store would have its database installed on a local computer completely isolated from the rest of the business. This creates major operational bottlenecks: branch cashiers would have no way of checking if a neighbouring outlet has excess stock, head office would be blind to real-time inventory depletion across branches, and a member who registers or renews their VIP status at one branch would not be recognized when visiting another outlet.

Furthermore, standalone systems cannot support modern omnichannel grocery features. Everyday retail operations, such as a customer placing an order online for in-store pickup or dispatching an order to third-party delivery partners like Grab and Foodpanda, depend entirely on live shared data. Without an online backend, the system cannot verify real-time stock levels before accepting an order, leading to frequent cancellations, overselling, and delayed fulfillments. A standalone setup would also force management to rely on manual end-of-day data exports from each store just to assemble sales figures, which introduces human error, duplicated effort, and outdated reports.

By implementing an online architecture backed by a centralized Oracle database, all branch POS registers, administrative consoles, and customer-facing web interfaces interact with a single source of truth over the network. This setup allows the database to handle concurrent read and write operations smoothly, using built-in locking mechanisms to prevent data conflicts when multiple cashiers or online shoppers access the same inventory items at the same time. Crucially, core business rules, including loyalty point calculations, voucher redemptions, promotional discount limits, and 7-day refund policies, are implemented and enforced directly at the database layer using Oracle PL/SQL stored procedures and triggers. This ensures that data validation rules remain consistent across all branches and channels, while giving management immediate access to network-wide sales, stock audits, and operational reports.


1.2 List of Modules

To support everyday grocery retail operations, omnichannel sales, and back-office management, the 88 Speedmart System is structured into five core functional modules:

1. Member and Loyalty Management Module
This module handles customer account registrations and manages standard accounts as well as annual RM12.00 VIP memberships. It automatically awards loyalty points for eligible purchases, monitors VIP renewal dates, and handles point redemptions for store discount vouchers and free delivery perks.

2. Inventory and Stock Procurement Module
This module oversees branch outlet records, supplier details, purchase orders (PO intake), and warehouse inventory replenishment. It tracks product batches and expiration dates to reduce spoilage, enforces storage limits for branches, and records damaged stock write-offs.

3. Order and Fulfillment Management Module
This module processes walk-in cash counter transactions and online grocery orders for both members and guest customers. It manages two fulfillment options, which are self-pickup at branch counters with secure collection codes, and home delivery dispatch through third-party logistics partners like Grab and Foodpanda, while settling payments and generating official tax invoices.

4. Promotion and Marketing Management Module
This module controls marketing campaigns, seasonal markdowns, and category discounts. It maintains promotion schedules, validates active date ranges, enforces price ceiling limits to protect profit margins, and recalculates shopping cart totals during checkout.

5. Refund and Return Management Module (Selected Additional Module)
This module manages post-purchase customer claims regarding damaged, missing, expired, or incorrect grocery items. It enforces business rules such as the 7-day claim submission window from the order date, routes claims to branch managers for review and approval, and tracks supplier liability for defective products.
